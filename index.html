History · Story · Tradition · Promise

Your wedding day is more than a gathering of family and friends. It is the beginning of a new chapter—and the ceremony is where you give that chapter meaning.

Every couple arrives with a story. The places that shaped you. The people who brought you here. The traditions you carry. The moments that changed you. The promises you want to make to one another.

Your ceremony should hold those things.

At The Heritage Vow, we create wedding ceremonies that bring your story together with the beauty and meaning of traditions that have endured across generations.

The ceremony is yours

Your story deserves to be part of the ceremony.

There is no universal script or template for a marriage ceremony.

Your ceremony should reflect who you are, where you came from, and—most importantly—the promise you are making to one another.

Before your ceremony is written, I want to know the two people who are standing there.

How you met. What you've overcome. What makes you laugh. Who you love. The people who helped shape you.

The promises you want to make—and the life you hope to build together.

Those details become the foundation of your ceremony.

The result isn't a collection of borrowed words. It is a ceremony written for you, spoken to you, and remembered as yours.
